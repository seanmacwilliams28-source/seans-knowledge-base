# How to Summon
* Most fusions require at least two monsters or more that are listed on the card to summon it and typically a spell card to enact this fusion
* There are some fusions however that can also be summoned through contact fusion, where you don't require a spell and just simply send the monsters listed on the card to the graveyard to summon it
* There is also some cards that allow you to fusion summon by tributing a monster instead of fusing 2 monsters together although typically these cards tend to make it a improper fusion summon. 
## History of Fusions
* Fusion monsters have been around since nearly the start of the game, Yu-Gi-Oh started in 1999 in Japan and the first introduction of fusions in the game was March 6, 1999. 
* Originally the first way to fusion summon monsters was through a spell card called polymerization, but nowadays in the current year there is many different ways to go about fusion summons.
### Play Style of Fusions
* Compared to Synchro, XYZ, and Link, Fusion's have a major weakness of usually needing a card to enact the fusion summon like polymerization or a similar card that allows the fusion summon to happen and then also needing the monsters required for the summon as well. 
* However a advantage for Fusions is that you can use the monsters from your hand and don't need to have them on the field to be used as Fusion material.
* Compared to older Fusions newer fusions tend to be less restrictive on the materials you need to fusion summon especially Fusions tied to a certain archetype.
