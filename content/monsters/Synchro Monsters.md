# How to Summon
* In order to Synchro summon you must need a Tuner and non tuner monster to summon a Synchro monster. 
* A tuner monster can be any normal or effect monster, even some synchro monster themselves can be a tuner as well and fusions
* A non tuner is any monster that doesn't say tuner on it, XYZ and Link monsters cannot be used for Synchro summons even though they are technically not tuner monsters but due to have a lack of level.
* In order to properly synchro summon your Tuner and non tuner or non tuners must equal the level of the monster you are trying to summon for example if you wanted to summon a 8star synchro the combined stars/levels of the tuner and non tuner must equal 8. 
## History of Synchro Summons
* Synchro monsters were first introduced in the TCG of August 2008. 
* They rose in popularity when they got their first booster set Duelist Genesis where a lot of staple synchro monsters that would be ran just about in everyone's extra deck back then, this included monsters like Stardust Dragon, Goyo Guardian, Colossal Fighter. 
* Before Synchro Monsters Yu-Gi-Oh was a much slower paced game with a lot of going back and forth between the two players, but when synchro monsters were released the competitive scene for the game became much faster paced. 
### Playstyle
* Usually decks that were focused on Synchro Summons would run monster heavy decks that would allow them to pretty much swarm the field with Tuners and Non tuners so they could pull off multiple synchro summons
* typically taking advantage of low level monsters so you can manipulate the levels to achieve the desired level of the synchro summon monster you are trying to summon. 
* Strategically wise Synchro monsters allowed for an explosive and rapid amount of power to be placed on board with relative ease and speed. 
* A lot of Synchro Monsters have potent effects that either happen when they are summoned or just while being on board.
