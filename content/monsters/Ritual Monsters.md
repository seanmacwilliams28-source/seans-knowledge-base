# How to Summon
* In order to do a ritual summon usually Ritual Spell card is needed that requires you to Tribute either monsters from your hand or field that equals the level of the monster or exceed the level of the monster. 
* Usually the Ritual spell is tied to the exact Ritual monster it is summoning, however there is a few Ritual spells that are used to summon all sorts of different Ritual monsters.
## Playstyle
* Due to being a part of the main deck Ritual monsters don't have the easy access of being available right a way to use like monsters in the extra deck. Thus involving one to either have to draw into it or search it out with the help of other cards. Thus decks that are focused on Ritual summoning usually run multiple cards to help search these monsters and spells related to summoning them.
* Ritual Reliant Decks typically run multiple copies of the spell cards and the Ritual monsters to have a greater chance of drawing into them earlier and the same with the cards to search them out as well. 
### History of Ritual Monsters
* Ritual Monsters were first introduced to the game in September 16, 2002 with the first monsters being Relinquished, Hungry Burger, and Crab Turtle.
* When Ritual Monsters were first introduced into the game it struggled on a competitive level due to the lack of searchers in the game or consistency issues. 
* Later on Ritual themed archetypes started to release and thus made Ritual more consistent and viable in the competitive scene.
