Another type of Monster you can run in your Extra deck

# How to Summon
* The way to summon an XYZ monster would usually be taking at least two or more monsters that were of the same level and overlaying them on top of each other for the XYZ monster that you wanted to summon. 
* Most XYZ were generic for the type of monsters you could use like for example needing two level 4 monsters to be used as the material to summon it.
* When you summon an XYZ you take the materials and place it under the XYZ monster that you bring out.
* There are even some XYZ monsters that require you to use another XYZ as its material this is typically called Xyz Evolution. you would place the new XYZ on top of the old one and it would gain the old XYZ and the materials it had as its materials
## History of XYZ
* The Xyz monsters were first introduced on July 8th, 2011 in the TCG
* These were the first monsters to be introduced into the game that did not have a level and instead referred to what would have been their level as ranks. 
* The name for this particular summon can act on a play of the word exceed and refers to the X, Y, and Z axes - reflecting the stacked cards. 
### Playstyle
* Much like Synchro based decks Xyz based decks typically tried to find ways to swarm the field with a bunch of monsters so they could do an exceed summon. however unlike Synchro based decks Xyz decks would try to get multiple monsters out of the same level to get the rank they desired to bring out.
* Most Xyz monsters have effects and usually the way to activate these effects is by detaching one of the materials that it has underneath it and sending that material to the graveyard.
