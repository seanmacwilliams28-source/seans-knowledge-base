# How to Summon
* Normal and Effect monsters could either be Normal summoned or special summoned. 
* Normal summon as long as its level 4 or lower you could just place it on the field from your hand although you only get 1 normal summon per turn. If it was level 5 or 6 the normal summon would require you to sacrifice a monster on board for it which would be called a tribute summon, and then if it was level 7 or higher it would require at least 2 sacrifices for its tribute summon unless stated otherwise on the card.
* For special summons you could special summon as many times as you wanted to. typically for a special summon its usually through the effect of the monster being a requirement to already be on the board or perhaps something dealing with revealing it in hand. 

## History of Normal/Effect Monsters
* since the Debut of the game Normal and Effect monsters were apart of the game.
* When the game first released Normal Monsters were the most common monsters in the game but as the game became more evolved less and less normal monsters were in the game and started shifting to more Effect monsters being in the game.
### Playstyle
* There isn't a whole lot of decks that use normal monsters or base their decks around them its more based around the effect monsters.
* there are several different ways to use effect monsters whether its using them to swarm the field, perhaps using its effect to destroy one of your opponents cards, or perhaps lock your opponent out of their strategy. Or perhaps even negating certain cards your opponent tries to activate.
* There are also some effect monsters that you don't even summon you instead keep them in your hand and wait to use them against a card your opponent activates or summons, these are typically referred as hand traps, even though they are effect monsters.
