Another type of monster you can run in your Extra deck

# How to Summon
* Most Link monsters have their requirements on them how to summon them for example 2 non link monsters or 2 monsters. Typically you send the monsters to grave and then bring out the Link monster to the Extra deck Monster zone.
* Link monsters range from Link 1 to Link 5. usually link 1 monsters require just 1 monster to bring out and well 5 will require 5 so pretty much what ever their link number is usually tells you how many monsters you will need. 
* In some cases you can use a lower link to help bring out a bigger link for example if wanted to bring out a link 4 monster but already had a Link 3 on board you could use the Link 3 and one other monster and that would also be acceptable to bringing out the Link 4 monster. 
* Also in the event you wanted to bring out more link monsters and you had one already in the extra deck monster zone you would only be able to summon where the Link monsters red arrows pointed to. 

## History of Link Monsters
* Link monsters are the newest addition to extra deck monsters they made their first introduction in July of 2017.
* When Link monsters were introduced there was a new rule change implemented to the game called Master Rule 4, which added an Extra monster zone to the field and changed how Extra Deck Summons would work.
* Link Monsters were the First monsters to be introduced to the game that had a lack of defense points and were also unable to be put into defense position due to this lack of defense points. 

### Playstyle
* Like Xyz and Synchro based decks Link based decks found ways to swarm the field with monsters so you can Link them off to summon the Link monsters.
* Link monsters sometimes had effects when they were Link summoned, but mostly they were used to benefit or weaken what the red arrows pointed to. 
* Due to trying to have Multiple Links on board positioning where your Link monsters went after being placed in the Extra deck zone was huge because if you misplaced a link that didn't point to another monster zone of yours you were pretty much locked out of being able to Link summon further. 
* There were even some decks that could essentially spam the board enough and position the link summons to pretty much make it to the other extra deck zone and lock the opponent out of the extra deck zone which would severely weaken the opponents board state on their turn.
